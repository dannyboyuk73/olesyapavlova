What to know

Your appointment

  • LocationSend Therapy Rooms, 175 Send Road, Send, Woking, Surrey GU23 7ET.
  • SundaysClinic days are Sundays — available slots are shown in the online booking calendar above.
  • What it involvesA relaxed, unhurried appointment: your concerns, a facial assessment, a review of your medical history, and an honest discussion of suitable options.
  • Consultation fee£50, redeemed in full against your treatment if you go ahead.
  • Same-day treatmentWhether any treatment can take place at the same visit depends on your assessment, and is discussed with you at the consultation.
