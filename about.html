About Olesya

Calm, honest, unhurried care

Mrs Olesya Pavlova is a GDC-registered dentist (GDC No. 156643) with over 20 years of clinical experience, now focused on dentist-led facial aesthetics in Send, near Woking.

Above all, her approach is calm, honest and unhurried. She'll only ever recommend what will genuinely help — and is just as happy to tell you when a treatment isn't right for you.

Clinical experience

Two decades in dentistry

Her career began with a dental degree in St. Petersburg, followed by roles at high-end London clinics including Harley Street, private practice in Geneva, and general and restorative dentistry across the UK.

Over 20 years of clinical experience as a dentist brings extensive knowledge of facial anatomy, careful patient assessment and precision injection techniques. She continues to study at the highest level — currently pursuing an MSc in Restorative Dentistry.

Approach to facial aesthetics

Subtle, natural, and led by assessment

Every plan begins with an individual consultation and a medical assessment of your face — never a fixed menu. The aim is subtle, natural-looking results: to look fresher, not different.

Changes in your appearance are discussed in person. Olesya will listen to your concerns, review your medical history and explain the options that may be appropriate for you — and she may advise that no treatment is needed. Read about the consultation.

Dentistry

A background in clinical dentistry

Olesya's facial-aesthetics work is grounded in clinical dentistry: detailed knowledge of facial anatomy, precise injecting, careful assessment and the governance of a registered healthcare professional.
