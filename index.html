Why a dentist

Why choose a dentist
for facial aesthetics?

Your face deserves medical judgement, not just a short course. Olesya brings over 20 years of clinical dental experience, daily injection skill, and detailed facial-anatomy knowledge to every treatment.

Injections, every single day

Administering injections is a core part of clinical dentistry. Over twenty years, Olesya has placed many thousands — bringing the anatomical familiarity and steady hand that only come from daily practice.

Trained in facial anatomy

The muscles, nerves, blood vessels and tissue planes of the face are studied in depth throughout dental training — and worked with every day in the chair. That knowledge guides safe, precise placement, and matters because poorly placed filler can cause serious complications.

A regulated healthcare professional

GDC-registered dentists work under strict clinical governance — ongoing education, annual appraisal and full professional indemnity. The NHS advises checking your practitioner's training, qualifications and insurance before any cosmetic procedure.

Prescribed, not just performed

Anti-wrinkle treatment is a prescription-only medicine that should be prescribed after a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. As a dentist, Olesya can properly assess your suitability and medical history before any treatment.

Guidance from the NHS on choosing a cosmetic practitioner: always check your practitioner's training, qualifications, insurance and what happens if something goes wrong.

About Olesya

Two decades of clinical
experience, applied to your face

Mrs Olesya Pavlova is a GDC-registered dentist with over 20 years of clinical experience, now focused on dentist-led facial aesthetics across Surrey. Her career began with a dental degree in St. Petersburg, followed by roles at high-end London clinics including Harley Street, private practice in Geneva, and general and restorative dentistry across the UK.

That background is exactly what makes her aesthetics work different: a daily, working knowledge of facial anatomy, thousands of injections placed, and the clinical governance that comes with being a registered healthcare professional. She is a trained aesthetic practitioner certified by the British Aesthetic Academy, and continues to study at the highest level — currently pursuing an MSc in Restorative Dentistry.

Above all, her approach is calm, honest and unhurried. She'll only ever recommend what will genuinely help — and is just as happy to tell you when a treatment isn't right for you.

FAQs

Common questions

Why choose a dentist for facial aesthetics?
Dentists train in detailed facial anatomy and administer precise injections every day. That anatomical knowledge, together with the clinical governance and aftercare standards of a GDC-registered healthcare professional, means your treatment is planned and delivered with medical judgement — not just a short course.
Do I need a consultation first?
Yes. Every treatment begins with a consultation to understand your goals, assess your suitability and medical history, and agree a personalised plan. Anti-wrinkle treatments are prescription-only medicines and are only provided after a medical assessment.
Do you offer anti-wrinkle treatments in Surrey?
Yes — anti-wrinkle consultations and treatments are available for areas such as frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet, following a medical assessment. Anti-wrinkle treatment is a prescription-only medicine, so it is only prescribed after a consultation, when it is appropriate for you.
Do you offer dermal fillers in Woking?
Yes. Dermal filler treatments — including lip refinement, cheek and volume restoration, and facial balancing — are available at the Send clinic, a short drive from Woking and Guildford.
How natural will the results look?
The aim is always subtle, natural-looking enhancement — refreshed, never overdone. Treatments are tailored to your features and agreed with you at consultation.
What aftercare do you provide?
You receive clear written aftercare and Olesya's direct number for any questions in the days that follow, with a review appointment to check your results if needed.
