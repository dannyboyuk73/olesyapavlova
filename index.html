About

Dentistry & aesthetics
with depth & dedication

Mrs Olesya Pavlova is a GDC-registered dentist with a performer number, practising across Surrey in fully private and mixed NHS/private settings. She combines genuine clinical skill with warm, patient-centred care — taking time to understand each patient's goals and building personalised treatment plans that put long-term oral health first.

Her clinical background spans general and restorative dentistry, endodontics, and the latest dental technologies: rotary endodontic systems, dental loupes, intraoral cameras, digital radiography, and dental microscopes — tools that allow for greater precision and less invasive treatment. She is currently pursuing a Master's in Restorative Dentistry with Tipton Training.

Alongside her dental practice, Olesya is a trained aesthetic practitioner certified by the British Aesthetic Academy. She offers a full range of anti-wrinkle and dermal filler treatments — from subtle upper-face refinements to full-face transformations — delivered with the anatomical precision and safety awareness that sets medically qualified practitioners apart.

Injections every day

Administering mouth injections is a core part of clinical dentistry. Olesya has placed thousands of them over twenty years — with the anatomical confidence and steady hand that only comes from daily practice.

Facial anatomy expert

The face is the dentist's domain. Every nerve, muscle, blood vessel and tissue plane in the face is covered in depth during dental training — and applied every day in the chair. That same knowledge protects you during every aesthetic treatment.

Highly regulated

GDC-registered dentists operate under some of the strictest clinical governance in UK healthcare — mandatory continuing education, rigorous annual appraisals, and full professional indemnity. These are standards beauty therapists are not held to.

UK law is changing

Under the Health and Care Act 2022, botulinum toxin in the UK must now be prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional. Dentists meet that requirement. Beauticians and hairdressers do not — and the consequences of going to an unqualified practitioner can be severe.
