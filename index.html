Why a dentist

Why choose a dentist
for facial aesthetics?

Olesya's approach is grounded in more than 20 years of clinical dentistry, detailed knowledge of facial anatomy, careful patient assessment and precision injection techniques — brought to every treatment.

Knowledge of facial anatomy

The muscles, nerves, blood vessels and tissue planes of the face are studied in depth throughout dental training and are central to clinical practice. That knowledge guides safe, precise placement — and matters, because poorly placed filler can cause serious problems.

Precision injection techniques

Precise, controlled injecting is a core clinical skill built over more than 20 years in dentistry. That translates into careful placement and a calm, steady, unhurried approach to your treatment.

Careful patient assessment

Every plan starts with a proper medical assessment — your goals, facial structure, medical history and suitability. Sometimes the honest answer is that a treatment isn't right for you, and you'll be told so.

A regulated healthcare professional

As a GDC-registered dentist (GDC No. 156643), Olesya maintains her professional development in accordance with the requirements of her registration. The NHS advises checking your practitioner's training, qualifications and insurance before any cosmetic procedure.

Guidance from the NHS on choosing a cosmetic practitioner: always check your practitioner's training, qualifications, insurance and what happens if something goes wrong.

Lines & expression
Concerned about lines?

Wrinkle-relaxing injections are a prescription treatment. That means the law doesn't allow clinics to advertise them by name or publish a price — and any clinic doing so is breaking the rules.

What it does mean: Olesya will assess your face, review your medical history, and discuss whether a prescription treatment is appropriate for you. If it is, she'll explain exactly what's involved and what it costs, before you commit to anything.

FAQs

Common questions

Why choose a dentist for facial aesthetics?
Olesya's approach is grounded in more than 20 years of clinical dentistry, detailed knowledge of facial anatomy and careful patient assessment. Together with the clinical governance and aftercare standards of a GDC-registered healthcare professional, that means your care is planned and delivered with medical judgement.
Do I need a consultation first?
Yes. Everything begins with a consultation to understand your goals, assess your suitability and medical history, and agree a personalised plan. A consultation may not always result in treatment — some concerns are better suited to other options, and Olesya will tell you honestly.
I'm concerned about lines and wrinkles — can you help?
The first step is always a personal consultation. Olesya will listen to your concerns, review your medical history and explain the options that may be appropriate for you. The right options, if any, can only be discussed following an individual clinical assessment. Learn about consultations or book a consultation.
Do you offer dermal fillers in Woking?
Yes. Dermal filler treatments — including lip filler, cheek filler, tear troughs, and facial balancing — are available at the Send clinic, a short drive from Woking and Guildford.
How natural will the results look?
The aim is always subtle, natural-looking enhancement — refreshed, never overdone. Treatments are tailored to your features and agreed with you at consultation.
What aftercare do you provide?
You receive clear written aftercare and Olesya's direct number for any questions in the days that follow, with a review appointment to check your results if needed.
