Consultations

Facial aesthetics consultation near Woking & Guildford

A calm, individual consultation with Mrs Olesya Pavlova, a GDC-registered dentist (No. 156643) in Send, Surrey — a short drive from Woking and Guildford.

A medical assessment, not a price list

Every treatment journey begins with an individual consultation. Olesya will listen to your concerns, review the relevant aspects of your medical history and discuss the options that may be appropriate for you. She may recommend an alternative approach, or advise that treatment is not suitable.

What happens

You'll talk through your goals, have a careful facial assessment, and agree an honest, personalised plan — including realistic expectations, aftercare and follow-up. There's no pressure to proceed, and a consultation does not guarantee treatment. Read more about the consultation.

Where

Consultations are held at Send Therapy Rooms, 175 Send Road, Send, Woking, Surrey GU23 7ET, serving Send, Woking, Guildford and the surrounding area. Appointments are available on selected Sundays.
