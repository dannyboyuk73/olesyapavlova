Tear trough filler in Surrey

A careful, honest approach to under-eye hollows with Mrs Olesya Pavlova, a GDC-registered dentist (No. 156643) in Send, near Woking and Guildford.

The treatment people ask about most — and the one that needs the most honesty

Tear trough filler places a small amount of soft dermal filler in the hollow between the lower eyelid and the cheek, softening the shadow that reads as tiredness. When the problem genuinely is a hollow, the change can be quietly transformative — not dramatic, just less tired.

It is also, honestly, the filler treatment with the least margin for error. The under-eye area has thin skin, little fat, and important structures close by, and it is less forgiving of over-filling than any other area of the face. That's why this page says more about who it isn't right for than most clinics choose to.

Price

£350 (1 ml). The price includes the volume shown. Your plan and final cost are confirmed at your consultation — £50, redeemed in full against your treatment.

Who it's right for

The best candidates have a true hollow — a groove you can see in even, overhead light — with good skin quality and little or no puffiness above it. If that's you, a conservative treatment can soften the shadow convincingly.

Who it's not right for

If the main concern is under-eye bags (puffiness rather than hollowness), filler tends to make things worse, not better. Very thin or crepey skin, significant skin laxity, a tendency to fluid retention or morning puffiness, and certain medical conditions all count against treatment. Some people are simply better served by sleep, allergy management, skincare — or, at the other end, by a surgical opinion. If that's the honest answer, it's the answer you'll get at consultation, and the £50 fee covers exactly that medical assessment.

What to expect

A consultation first, always — assessment in different light and angles, medical history, and a frank conversation about whether you're a good candidate. If treatment goes ahead: a conservative amount of product, placed slowly, with a follow-up review where clinically appropriate. Results are assessed over two weeks as any swelling settles. Written aftercare comes home with you.

Why a dentist?

Precision injection work in delicate, high-consequence anatomy is a dentist's daily craft. Over 20 years of clinical experience — and a professional obligation to advise against treatment where it isn't right — is precisely what you want near the eyes.
