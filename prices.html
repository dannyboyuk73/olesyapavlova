What's included

Every treatment includes

Your £50 consultation (redeemed in full against your treatment), the treatment itself with the volume shown, written aftercare guidance to take home, and a follow-up review where clinically appropriate.

Payment and appointments

If a deposit is needed to secure your appointment, this is explained to you when you book. Cancellation and rescheduling terms are confirmed when your appointment is arranged.

All prices include the volume shown. If more than one area is treated in a session, the plan and total are agreed and confirmed with you before anything begins. Prices are a guide — your treatment plan and final costs are confirmed at your consultation.
