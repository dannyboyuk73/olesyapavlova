Lip filler

Lip filler in Surrey

Subtle, natural-looking lip enhancement with Mrs Olesya Pavlova, a GDC-registered dentist (No. 156643) in Send, near Woking and Guildford.

Natural, considered enhancement

Lip filler uses a gel-based dermal filler to enhance the shape, border and balance of the lips. Olesya's approach is deliberately subtle — refining and defining rather than over-filling — so the result looks like you, on a good day. £310 for 1 ml — the price includes the volume shown; your plan and final cost are confirmed at your consultation, which is £50, redeemed in full against your treatment.

What to expect

Every treatment begins with an individual consultation to understand what you'd like, assess what will suit your features, and review your medical history and suitability. There's no pressure to proceed, and Olesya will give you an honest view of what will — and won't — help.

Why a dentist?

Over 20 years of clinical experience as a dentist brings extensive knowledge of facial anatomy, careful patient assessment and precision injection techniques. More about Olesya.
