Dermal fillers

Dermal fillers near Woking

Dentist-led dermal filler treatments in Send — a short drive from Woking and Guildford — with Mrs Olesya Pavlova, a GDC-registered dentist (No. 156643).

Subtle, natural-looking results

Dermal fillers are gel-based treatments used to restore volume lost with age, soften folds, refine the lips and gently balance features. The aim is always subtle and natural — to look fresher, not different. Every treatment is planned around your face at an individual consultation.

Treatments available

Cheek filler

Restores lost volume or enhances cheekbone definition for a lifted, refreshed look. £295 (1 ml).

Lip filler

Natural-looking shape, border and volume, tailored to your features. £310 (1 ml).

Tear troughs

Softens hollows and shadows beneath the eyes for a less tired appearance. £350 (1 ml).

Nasolabial & marionette lines

Softens the folds around the nose and mouth. £295 (1 ml) per area.

Chin & jawline filler

Adds definition and balance to the chin and jawline profile. Chin £295 (1 ml); jawline priced at your consultation.

Non-surgical rhinoplasty

Refines the appearance of the nose with precise filler placement. £380.
