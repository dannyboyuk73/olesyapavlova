The first step

Your consultation

Everything begins with a proper conversation and a medical assessment of your face — not a price list. It's where we understand your goals, check what's suitable, and agree an honest, personalised plan together.

What to expect

A calm, unhurried assessment

Every treatment journey begins with an individual consultation. Olesya will listen to your concerns, review the relevant aspects of your medical history and discuss the options that may be appropriate for you. She may recommend an alternative approach or advise that treatment is not suitable.

The consultation is £50, redeemed in full against your treatment. If you go ahead, the fee comes off the cost of your treatment; if the honest advice is that treatment isn't right for you, the fee covers the medical assessment you've received.

Your consultation is a relaxed, private appointment with Olesya herself. There's no pressure and no obligation, and you are never expected to decide on the day.

As a GDC-registered dentist (GDC No. 156643) with over 20 years of clinical experience, Olesya brings extensive knowledge of facial anatomy, careful patient assessment and precision injection techniques to every plan. Where a prescription is required, the assessment is carried out face to face before anything is prescribed.

01
Understanding your goals

We talk through what you'd like to change and why, and what a natural, subtle result would look like for you.

02
Facial assessment

A careful look at your facial structure, proportions and skin — assessing what will genuinely help and what won't.

03
Medical history & suitability

We review your medical history, medications and any previous treatments to check whether a given option is safe and appropriate for you.

04
An honest, personalised plan

You'll get clear, unbiased advice and a plan agreed with you — including realistic expectations, aftercare and follow-up. Some options are prescription-only medicines and can only be considered after this assessment.

What we might discuss

Possible options

Every plan is individual. Depending on your goals and what's suitable for you, the options discussed at consultation may include:

Dermal fillers

Restoring lost volume, softening folds, refining the lips, and subtly balancing features such as the cheeks, chin and jawline.

Skin & ageing concerns

Assessing lines, folds and volume loss, and discussing which approaches — if any — are appropriate for your skin and stage of ageing.

Prescription treatments

Where relevant and suitable, prescription-only options may be discussed. These are never sold off the shelf — they can only follow a medical assessment and, where appropriate, a prescription.

A consultation may not result in treatment. If something isn't right for you — or wouldn't give the result you're hoping for — Olesya will tell you plainly. Honest advice is the whole point.

If you would like to read balanced, factual information about specific prescription treatments before your appointment, you can view our treatment information page. It is provided for information only and is not an offer of treatment.
